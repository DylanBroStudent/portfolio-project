About

Hi! My name's Dylan and I'm a game developer based in Nelson, New Zealand.

Outside of my work as an artist and developer, I design 3D prints, fly drones, read comics, and play a lot of videogames.

Details

I arrived in New Zeland when I was nine. I had lived with my family on a 38ft yacht called Music my entire life until that point. The boat was built by my father in New Zealand several years before I was born.

A couple months after Music began its circumnavigation of the world I was born in South Port, Australia. Music wouldn't return to New Zealand until almost a decade later, with me onboard.
